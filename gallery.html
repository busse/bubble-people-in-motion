Phase 1Mar7 – 11 Mar 2026

Decomposing the paintings

Six vertical 1080×1920 reels, 35 seconds to almost 7 minutes long, made by the earlier decompose-paintings project (Python, GPT-4o, SAM2, gpt-image-1). Takes 1–3 cut each bubble person out of the paper, often leaving a black silhouette where the figure was. From take 4 the approach switched to generating the parts, and the reels add collectable trading cards with names such as “Guardian” and “Star Gazer.” The deck tells this part in full.

Previews show the first 20 seconds of each take.

Phase 2Apr24 Apr 2026

First Veo tests

Three 8-second clips at 720×1280, with the Veo watermark in the corner. Veo animates the whole painting, paper edge and all, so nothing can be keyed. The guitar busker and the arcade player in these tests come back a few weeks later as pipeline subjects.

Phase 3May7 – 8 May 2026

The bpd pipeline

A TypeScript CLI. Gemini 2.5 Flash describes each bubble person in the painting, then that description, a shared style prompt, a hand-written action prompt and a locked green-screen tail go to Veo 3.1 with the painting as reference. Output is 8 seconds at 4K, the only length Veo allows at that resolution. A fast-tier clip costs $2.40 and takes 5 to 9 minutes.

Prompts6×longest vs first draft

Directions got longer as the failures taught them

Words in each action prompt, in the order the paintings were first run. Gold bars are earlier drafts recovered from git. Every prompt is also wrapped in the 510-word style prompt and the 194-word green-screen tail. Most of the added words are guards against things Veo did in an earlier take: making figures rounder and fuller, rendering the scene meant for compositing, cropping heads.
